About / Research profile

Greetings!

I am currently a PhD student at the DRAGON Lab , The University of Tokyo, supervised by Associate Professor Moju Zhao . I am into aerial robotics, with a focus on motion planning research, from foundational algorithms to real-world applications.

I obtained my MSc and BEng degree from Beihang University .

Interests

  • Aerial Robotics
  • Motion Planning
  • Optimization
  • Embodied AI
